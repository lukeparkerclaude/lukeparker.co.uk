Burnham Says Brexit Has 'Done More Harm Than Good'. Is Rejoining the Real Plan?

At Labour's conference, the Prime Minister said Brexit has 'done more harm than good' and promised to set out 'options' on the EU. He has not ruled rejoining out. Voters deserve to know before, not after, an election.

On 29 September, at Labour's conference in Liverpool, Prime Minister Andy Burnham said Brexit “has done more harm than good” and that the UK-EU relationship is not “good enough.” He promised to “lay out to the country” the options for Britain's future relationship with the EU.

Let's be honest about what that means. When a Prime Minister who walked into Downing Street without a general election starts talking about “options” on rejoining, voters are right to sit up.

What Was Actually Said

Burnham told conference: “There are different options, and I will see if we can find consensus around one.” According to City AM, his spokesman said he will set out the options around the time of the EU summit, which has no confirmed date, and that “we will put this on the ballot paper at the next election.” To be fair, that line came from the spokesman, not from Burnham himself, and he has not committed to rejoining.

The same report says the existing red lines on the customs union and single market will hold until the next election, but the spokesman would not rule out a rejoin pledge. Sadiq Khan is the only senior figure named as backing rejoining outright, while Wes Streeting and Peter Kyle have pushed for crossing the customs union red line.

The Democratic Point

In 2016 the country voted to leave. Whatever you think of how the last decade has gone, that was the biggest vote this country has ever held. My honest read: a government that wants to unwind it should say so plainly and ask for permission, not drift there through “options” and summits.

Euronews reports Reform's own position is simple: Farage argues that hope for control was “completely betrayed” by both Conservative and Labour governments, and says we need to get back control of our borders.

Where Reform UK Stands

Reform UK says Brexit should be made to work, not reversed. We want proper control of our borders, our laws and our money. If Labour is heading towards rejoining, they should tell the public now, with a clear manifesto promise and a clear answer on what it would cost and what it would give away.

Sources: City AM, ‘Burnham opens door to rejoining EU as he rips into Brexit’ (29 September 2026): conference quotes, spokesman statements on options, EU summit and ballot paper, party reactions. Euronews (12 October 2026) for Farage's ‘completely betrayed’ quote and Burnham's Brexit remarks.
