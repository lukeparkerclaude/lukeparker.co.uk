Winter Fuel Clawback: Pensioners Face £33 a Month HMRC Deduction

The Winter Fuel Payment goes to every pensioner again this year — then HMRC claws it straight back from anyone earning over £35,000, with monthly deductions rising to £33 by 2027-28 as two years of payments are recovered in one go.

On the surface, this looks like good news: every state pensioner will receive a Winter Fuel Payment again this year, restoring the universal system Labour tore up in its first year in office. Look at the small print, though, and pensioners on modest incomes are about to discover that what the government gives with one hand, HMRC quietly takes back with the other — and the clawback is about to get bigger.

Universal Again, But With a Catch

Under the new arrangement, every pensioner receives the payment automatically. Those who have reached state pension age but are under 80 will see an increase of £100, taking their payment to £300, up from £200. It sounds like a straightforward win for pensioners. But anyone earning £35,000 or more a year is required to repay it to HMRC through their tax code — effectively means-testing the payment through the back door, after ministers spent the best part of a year defending the front-door version of means-testing that caused such an outcry.

The Clawback Doubles

Here's the detail that hasn't had the attention it deserves. For the 2026/27 tax year, pensioners above the threshold with a typical £200 payment will pay around £17 extra a month through their tax code. In 2027/28, that rises to roughly £33 a month, because HMRC will be recovering two years' worth of Winter Fuel Payments through a single tax code adjustment. A pensioner doing everything right — working, saving, staying just above an arbitrary income line — ends up handing back double the following year, buried in a tax code change most will never fully understand until the money is already gone from their pay.

This is the same pattern Reform UK has criticised in Labour's broader tax approach: complexity dressed up as fairness, with pensioners on modest incomes bearing the administrative confusion while the underlying sums are barely different from simple means-testing.

Deadlines Pensioners Need to Know

Pensioners who want to opt out of receiving the payment altogether — rather than have it clawed back automatically later — have a narrow window. The online opt-out form must be submitted by 11:59pm on 20 September 2026. Anyone opting out by phone through the Winter Fuel Payment helpline must do so by 6pm on 18 September 2026. Miss both deadlines, and the payment arrives automatically, with the clawback following through your tax code months later.

What Reform UK Would Do

Reform UK would restore a simple, universal Winter Fuel Payment without a hidden clawback mechanism that punishes pensioners for earning a modest income above an arbitrary threshold. Pensioners who worked hard their whole lives deserve a system they can understand, not a tax code adjustment that quietly doubles year on year. If the government wants to means-test the payment, it should say so honestly — not disguise it as a tax deduction buried in a payslip.
