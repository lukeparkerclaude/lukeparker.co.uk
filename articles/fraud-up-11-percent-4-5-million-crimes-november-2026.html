Fraud Up 11% to 4.5 Million: The Crime Wave Westminster Ignores

Fraud is now the crime most likely to hit you, and it is growing. The official Crime Survey estimates 4.5 million fraud incidents a year, up 11%, while computer misuse jumped 23%. Yet you rarely hear a minister talk about it.

If you have had a dodgy text pretending to be your bank, a fake parcel delivery message, or a call from someone claiming to be HMRC, you are not alone. Fraud is now one of the biggest crime problems in the country, and the official figures show it is getting worse.

What the Figures Say

The Office for National Statistics' latest Crime in England and Wales bulletin, covering the year ending June 2026, estimates 4.5 million fraud incidents, a rise of 11%. Computer misuse, which covers hacking and viruses, rose 23% to 817,000 incidents. The Crime Survey puts total headline crime at 9.7 million incidents. Put those together and fraud is nearly half of it.

Credit Where It's Due

I want to be fair, because people deserve the full picture. Some police-recorded crime fell over the same period: knife or sharp instrument offences fell 6% to 48,615, and shoplifting fell 2% to 519,516. That is welcome, and credit goes to officers on the ground. But shoplifting is still at more than half a million recorded offences, and the ONS itself warns that police-recorded figures are not a reliable guide to overall trends. Sexual offences recorded by police rose 4% to 218,994.

The Crime Nobody Owns

Let's be honest about why fraud keeps growing. It is the crime that falls between the cracks. Victims are often elderly, the criminals are often overseas, and because nobody is stabbed or burgled, it never gets the headlines. My honest read is that successive governments have quietly accepted fraud as the price of modern life. I don't accept that, and neither should you.

Where Reform UK Stands

Treat fraud as the serious crime it is. Reform UK wants proper resources for fraud investigation, banks and tech firms held to account for the scams running through their platforms, and tough sentences for those who prey on pensioners and families. Every one of those 4.5 million incidents is a real person who has been robbed. They deserve a government that takes it seriously.

Source: Office for National Statistics, Crime in England and Wales: year ending June 2026 (released 22 October 2026). Crime Survey for England and Wales: fraud 4.5m incidents (up 11%), computer misuse 817,000 (up 23%), headline crime 9.7m. Police recorded crime: knife or sharp instrument offences 48,615 (down 6%), shoplifting 519,516 (down 2%), sexual offences 218,994 (up 4%).
