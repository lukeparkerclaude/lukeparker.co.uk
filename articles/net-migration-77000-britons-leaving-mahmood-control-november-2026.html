Net Migration Falls to 77,000 — So Why Are 245,000 Britons Packing Their Bags?

The ONS says net migration fell to 77,000 in the year to June 2026, the lowest outside Covid since 1997. The Home Secretary says the government has restored control. The fall is real, and it is welcome. But look underneath the headline and the picture is not the victory lap Labour wants it to be.

Let's start with what is true, because accuracy matters. Net migration has fallen, and fallen a long way. That is welcome, and it happened because the public demanded it.

What the ONS Said

The Office for National Statistics estimates net migration of 77,000 in the year to June 2026, the lowest level outside the Covid pandemic since 1997. An estimated 738,000 people immigrated and 661,000 emigrated. The ONS says the fall has been driven by fewer people arriving from outside the EU for work, study and asylum.

Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood was quick off the mark: “This Government has restored control. We have brought net migration to 77,000, down 92% from its peak under the Conservatives.”

Look Under the Bonnet

A net figure is arrivals minus departures. And one of the biggest departures is our own people. The ONS estimates 245,000 British nationals left the UK in the year to June 2026, while only 109,000 came back or arrived. In fairness, the ONS warns the British figures are subject to greater uncertainty and revision, and its main explanation for the overall fall is fewer non-EU arrivals rather than Britons leaving. But a country where well over twice as many of its own citizens leave as arrive should not be throwing a party.

Meanwhile 550,000 people still arrived from outside the EU in a single year. That is not a small town. That is a city the size of Manchester.

Control Is More Than a Number

My honest read: when young Britons are leaving because they cannot afford a home, cannot find a decent job, or simply no longer believe the country is heading the right way, a lower net figure is partly a symptom, not just a success. Real control means deciding who comes, removing those with no right to be here, and building a country our own young people want to stay in.

Where Reform UK Stands

Reform UK believes immigration should be low, controlled and in the national interest, every year, not just in a good set of statistics. That means a firm cap, ending the abuse of the asylum system, stopping the boats for good, and putting British workers and British families first so that our own people have a future worth staying for.

Source: ONS migration estimates for the year ending June 2026, as reported by ITV News, 26 November 2026 (net 77,000; immigration 738,000; emigration 661,000; British nationals 245,000 out / 109,000 in; non-EU immigration 550,000; Shabana Mahmood and ONS Sarah Crofts quotes). Primary source
